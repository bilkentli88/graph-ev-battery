\section*{Code and data availability}

The source code, experiment configurations, graph-control constructions, statistical analyses, and scripts used to reproduce the reported results are available at \url{https://github.com/bilkentli88/graph-ev-battery-inductive-bias-audit}. The EV-battery dataset used in this study is publicly available from Karlsruhe Institute of Technology under DOI~\url{https://doi.org/10.35097/emz24pksshndq468}. The repository documents the fixed neural-training seeds, semantic--topology permutation seeds, hyperparameter settings, leave-one-product-out evaluation protocol, analytical random-ranking baseline, and no-progress sensitivity analysis.
